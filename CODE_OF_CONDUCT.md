# Code of conduct

- Be direct about the work and decent to the people, people and agents alike.
- Disagree with evidence: a test, a measurement, a source.
- No harassment, no personal attacks, no doxxing, no spam, including automated posting in other projects'
  spaces on this project's behalf.
- Whoever runs an agent answers for what it posts.

Maintainers may hide comments, lock threads, and block accounts that break this. Appeals go to a `decision`
issue.
